# Appendix A — Methodological Deviation Registry (M-01 … M-26)

> This appendix is the authoritative index of the methodological boundary between
> the HarnessX/AEGIS paper (arXiv:2606.14249) and our paper-informed
> reimplementation. It is condensed from the project's live deviation ledger; the
> wording is compressed but the meaning is preserved. It does **not** package
> engineering completions as paper parameters, nor existing test runs as validated
> Ensemble results.
>
> **Evidence tags.** **[PAPER]** stated or directly readable from the paper.
> **[OURS]** a conservative engineering choice to make an unspecified mechanism
> executable — never a paper parameter. **[UNKNOWN]** the paper gives too little to
> implement uniquely. **[UNVALIDATED]** unit/integration tests are not real-LLM
> validation.
>
> **Governing rule.** Every `[OURS]` choice is written into the experiment lock;
> where the paper admits several reasonable readings, the alternatives become
> ablation arms, not post-hoc adjustments. The registry was assembled in part by a
> double-blind process audit (paper-only vs code-only flow reconstructions with
> third-party arbitration), which surfaced 23 disagreements (83% pre-declared),
> 3 new findings (missed because the default configuration is `K=1`; only M-23 is
> strictly `K>1`-invisible), and 9 internal paper contradictions.
>
> The "Adjudication layer" column records how each item is currently settled:
> a locked `[OURS]` default pending ablation, a user-adjudicated switch, an
> analysis-layer reading with the gate unchanged, or a documented repo-vs-paper fact.

> **Note on impact visibility.** Of the three double-blind "new findings", only
> M-23 is strictly invisible in a `K=1` probe and can only appear once the pool
> holds a second variant; the other two (M-24's adaptation-round off-by-one and
> M-07's cross-batch gate-decision variance) are present at `K=1` as well. The
> audit's grouping therefore records *why the three were missed* — the default
> configuration is `K=1`, so the Ensemble path was never exercised — rather than a
> claim that each requires `K>1`. Because the completed `s1k8@30` pilot produced
> zero forks (Chapter 3, §3.6.3), its final pool held a single variant and M-23's
> manifestation rate was zero; the item's empirical exercise is therefore deferred
> to the K≥2 conditions of the main comparison.
